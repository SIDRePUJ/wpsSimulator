# Formal loan protocol (model description)

A household asks for a formal loan when its money is at or below the liquidity threshold mu (COP 300,000) and it has no active loan. The bank approves a request only if the household has no active loan and the bank's lending capital is strictly greater than the loan amount kappa (COP 2,000,000); the capital is a fixed lending ceiling that is checked at approval and is not modified by loans or payments. On approval the bank returns kappa (the amount credited to the household); on denial it returns 0. The loan is repaid in n = 12 equal monthly installments of kappa / n, without interest. When asked for the current term, the bank returns the installment kappa / n for a household with an active loan and 0 otherwise. A payment is accepted (True) only if the household has an active loan and the amount equals the installment; otherwise it is rejected (False) and nothing changes. After the n-th accepted payment the loan is closed, so the household has no active loan and may borrow again.
