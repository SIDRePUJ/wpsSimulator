# Prospective post-pilot scenario register

**Status:** design lock for the **next** multi-date experiment, not a claim of preregistration before all evidence. The three-family, single-day, 50%-budget technical pilot and its tiny rule contrasts were already inspected. Do not reuse them as confirmatory findings. No multi-date rule outcomes have been viewed under this register.

## Estimand and model boundary

For the same rainfall proxy, fixed request schedule, synthetic UPA cohort, seasonal source budget and random seed, compare `EQUAL_PER_HECTARE` and `SMALL_PLOT_FLOOR` with `PROPORTIONAL_DEMAND`. Primary paired outcomes are total modeled rice tonnes, unweighted Gini of **UPA relative production loss**, and the smallest-UPA-area-quartile 90th-percentile relative loss. Report absolute-tonne-loss Gini and source conservation as secondary outcomes. These are modeled production losses, **not** income inequality, observed district effects or historical policy evaluation.

Use `-Dwps.water.horizon=SEASONAL_ENTITLEMENT`: the complete seasonal request roster is known before assigning plot entitlements; each plot's entitlement is then spread over its dates. The previous `ROUND_CHRONOLOGICAL` horizon has early-date priority and is a separate temporal institution, not an interchangeable implementation of the same rule. The small-plot floor is plot-based; any UPA-equity effect is an outcome to measure, not part of its definition.

The simulator has an FAO-56-style root-zone depletion/ET-stress balance, **not** explicit flooded-paddy storage, saturation and percolation. [FAO's paddy irrigation method](https://www.fao.org/4/s2022e/s2022e08.htm) includes the omitted processes. Therefore request depths below are **scenario root-zone supplements**, not paddy water requirements or measurements for María La Baja. Adding a calibrated paddy module would be a different, larger study.

## Sequential minimum, not a combinatorial wish list

1. **Input gate:** demonstrate 12 stable family/farm/plot assignments, eligible planting/harvest windows, all-season request dates, exact gross-demand and stock arithmetic, and one-date equivalence of the new horizon. A controlled 2×12, 24-land-cell subset of `world.100.json` supplies exactly twelve two-cell farms; it is **synthetic topology**, not a local cadastral map. Two unforced discoveries gave identical ownership/planting traces but different crop portfolios. Two full-year `RICE_ONLY` one-day runs exited 0 with 12/12 farms, 48/48 water plots, 24/24 eligible rice harvests and byte-identical water/climate/yield outputs. The frozen [plot roster](data/derived/world24_plot_roster.csv), [crop windows](data/derived/world24_crop_windows.csv) and two [rainfall-conditioned schedules](data/derived/world24-weekly-2014-guarded.csv) reproduce the 4/5/3 UPA composition. One corrected multi-date 2014/0.35 proportional full-year run exited 0 with water 48/48, 384 applied deliveries, harvest 24/24 and climate 2,904/2,904 rows; its strict join admitted 24 plots. This clears a **single-scenario multi-date wiring gate**, not the full two-weather/two-scarcity/three-rule/two-seed technical screen or any policy ranking.
2. **Technical screen:** run two rainfall proxies × two scarce ratios × three rules × two fixed seeds (24 full-year runs). Require exit zero, complete farm/water/yield/climate audits and the strict [joined-results gate](RESULTS_PROTOCOL.md), identical forcing and eligible UPA cohort across rules, and exact source conservation. Any failure is an implementation blocker, not a zero outcome.
3. **Minimum analysis:** only if the screen passes, extend the same central cells to at least ten paired seeds. Include the 1.00 diagnostic sparingly to confirm no-scarcity agreement; do not count it as evidence of an equity–efficiency trade-off. Use a paired seed-level interval rather than a single winner label.
4. **Focused sensitivity:** recompute `Ym`/`Ky` offline; run efficiency and weekly-depth endpoints initially at two paired seeds, then expand only if qualitative conclusions are unstable. Check a second synthetic area composition without calling either population representative. State every unexpanded axis and its consequence.

## Publication and stopping rules

- Report a conditional trade-off only if its direction and material magnitude survive the registered scarce ratios, rainfall proxies, paired seeds and feasible uncertainty bounds. If changes remain as tiny as in the one-day pilot, report a **null/inconclusive institutional contrast**, not a ranked policy recommendation.
- The external validation ceiling remains low: no observed district water stocks/releases, plot deliveries, local station series or water-deficit/yield pairs have been acquired. [CNA area classes](data/derived/rice_area_distribution.json) constrain composition; EVA checks broad production plausibility only. A simulated UPA is not a sampled observed household.
- Do not claim flooded-paddy hydrology, historical district governance, income/welfare distribution or climate-driven reservoir shortage. Meteorological rainfall and seasonal source stock are independent scenario inputs.
- Keep the work distinct from the IEEE TCSS irrigation-access/on/off analysis and the Journal of Simulation simulator-methods paper: the contribution is the finite-source **institutional allocation comparison** under explicit physical and empirical limits.
